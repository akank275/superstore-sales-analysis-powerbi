# 📊 Superstore Sales Analysis & Power BI Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** built to analyze Superstore sales performance, profitability, product categories, regions, cities, and states.

The project demonstrates practical skills in **Power BI, Power Query, DAX, data visualization, and business analysis**.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The goal of this project is to analyze sales and profitability data and convert it into an interactive dashboard that helps identify:

- Overall sales and profit performance
- Best-performing product categories and sub-categories
- Regional and state-level performance
- Monthly profit trends
- Sales and quantity distribution
- High-performing cities
- Impact of discounts on profitability

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The dashboard was created to answer important business questions such as:

1. What is the total sales and profit generated?
2. Which product categories generate the most sales?
3. Which sub-categories perform the best?
4. Which regions and states are most profitable?
5. How does profit change over time?
6. Which cities have the highest sales quantity?
7. How are discounts affecting profitability?
8. Which areas require further business attention?
